# Sultsinat – Traditional Karelian Rye Flatbreads

Sultsinat are a traditional Karelian dish made from very thin rye flatbreads filled with a soft filling. They are best served warm and eaten by hand.

## Ingredients

### Dough

* 200 ml cold water
* 1 tsp salt
* about 400 ml rye flour
* about 100 ml wheat flour (makes the dough easier to roll out)

### Filling

Possible fillings include:

* soft semolina porridge
* barley porridge
* smooth mashed potatoes
* rice porridge
* and many others you dare to test...

The porridges are preferably cooked with milk and lightly seasoned with salt. 
Rice porridge is a newer addition as a sultsina filling than the more traditional semolina, barley, and potato fillings.

### For brushing

* 50–100 g butter
* A splash of milk or water

## Instructions

1. Mix the cold water and salt. Gradually add the rye flour and wheat flour and knead until you have a firm, smooth dough.

2. Shape the dough into a log and divide it into small, equal-sized pieces.

3. Roll each piece into a very thin, round flatbread, approximately the size of a small plate. Use rye flour to prevent the dough from sticking.

4. Brush off excess flour and cook each flatbread quickly on both sides on a very hot, dry cast-iron pan or griddle.
   Do not use fat for frying. The surface should develop small brown spots.

6. Immediately after cooking, brush **only one side** of each flatbread with melted butter mixed with a little milk or water.

7. Stack the flatbreads in pairs with the **buttered sides facing each other**. Cover them with a clean kitchen towel so that they soften and remain flexible.

## Filling and folding

1. Place one softened flatbread on a plate with the **unbuttered side facing upwards**.

2. Spread a thin, even layer of warm porridge or smooth mashed potatoes over the unbuttered side, leaving a little space around the edges.

3. Fold the left and right sides towards the centre so that the edges almost meet.

4. Fold the sultsina once more lengthwise in half.

5. The **buttered side should now be on the outside**.

6. Eat the sultsina warm, by hand, with the **buttered side towards your lips**.

Sultsinat are best eaten immediately while the flatbread is still soft and the filling is warm.
