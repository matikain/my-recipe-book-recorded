## Ingredients

### Dough

* 200 ml cold water
* 1 tsp salt
* about 400 ml rye flour
* about 100 ml wheat flour (makes the dough easier to roll out) [[1](https://careliagrill.com/fi/blogs/recipes/open-fire-sultsinat), [2](https://www.k-ruoka.fi/reseptit/sultsinat), [3](https://www.karjalainennuorisoliitto.fi/toiminta/tuunaamunperinneruoka/huhtikuu/)]

### Filling (porridge)

* Cooked, soft semolina, rice, or barley porridge, preferably made with milk
* A pinch of salt [[1](https://foodbytwins.com/sultsinat-mummon-resepti-pohjois-karjalasta/), [2](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultsina), [3](https://www.k-ruoka.fi/reseptit/sultsinat), [4](https://careliagrill.com/fi/blogs/recipes/open-fire-sultsinat)]

### For brushing

* 50–100 g butter
* A splash of milk or water
